# Pre-registration P2, addendum 3: three researcher-supplied PDFs

**Date:** 2026-11-25. This addendum applies section 2 of the registered
`docs/PREREGISTRATION_P2.md`. It changes nothing in the hypothesis, the criteria
or the falsifiers.

## Outcome

None of the three PDFs is a heat-capacity measurement on an insulating single
crystal. **No qualifying dataset.** The target from addendum 2, Barucci et al.
(2001), J. Low Temp. Phys. 123, 303, DOI 10.1023/A:1017555615150, is still
needed.

## Files and decisions

The researcher supplied the files from `~/Downloads/`. They are not committed:
none qualifies, and they are publisher or arXiv copies. A separate agent
identified each from its title block and read only methods sections.

| File | Identity | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| `barucci_2008.pdf` | Barucci, Lolli, Risegari, Ventura, "Measurement of thermal conductivity of the supports of CUORE cryostat", Cryogenics 48, 166 (2008), DOI 10.1016/j.cryogenics.2008.03.018 | fails: thermal conductivity of Ti6Al4V and 316LN steel, not heat capacity of an insulating crystal |
| `0212053v1.pdf` | CUORE Collaboration (Arnaboldi et al.), "CUORE: A Cryogenic Underground Observatory for Rare Events", arXiv:hep-ex/0212053v1 (2002) | fails 5 (and 6-9): detector proposal; the TeO2 heat capacity is cited, not measured. | `1412.3619v1.pdf` | Marx, Hoess, Uhlig, "Dry Dilution Refrigerator for Experiments on Quantum Effects in the Microwave Regime", arXiv:1412.3619v1 (2014) | fails: apparatus paper, no sample |

The researcher also pasted a chatbot (Gemini) summary of Barucci, Ventura,
Risegari et al., "Very-low temperature specific heat of Torlon", Cryogenics 46,
767 (2006), DOI 10.1016/j.cryogenics.2006.05.006. It fails criterion 1 (Torlon is
an amorphous polymer).
